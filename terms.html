Terms of use

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

TerraTrace is a prototype for comparing satellite spectral indices. By using this site, you understand that it is provided for demonstration and exploratory analysis, without guarantees of availability, accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose, or uninterrupted service.

Data and outputs

Real-data mode queries the Element 84 Earth Search STAC API and reads Sentinel-2 Level-2A cloud-optimized GeoTIFF assets in your browser. The selected scene, cloud cover, acquisition date, processing choices and data gaps affect the output. Sample mode uses generated demonstration values and is not satellite data.

Indices and change classes are not verified environmental findings. Results do not prove cause, climate change, water availability, water quality, land ownership, legal status, engineering suitability, or financial return. Validate results with qualified experts and independent sources before making decisions.

This site may request resources from Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr, Element 84 Earth Search, Sentinel-2 COG storage, and OpenStreetMap tile servers. Their availability, terms, and privacy practices are controlled by those providers. Map tiles are subject to OpenStreetMap attribution and usage policies. Sentinel data use is subject to applicable Copernicus data terms.

The site is provided as-is. APIs, imagery assets, map tiles, and external libraries can fail or change. We may change or remove prototype functionality without notice. Do not upload confidential or sensitive information into the study area label.
