Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

This static prototype does not offer accounts, does not ask for a contact profile, and does not intentionally store study areas or analysis results on a server. Analysis and report generation run in your browser. The site does not include analytics or advertising trackers at the time of writing.

Requests to third parties

When you use the site, your browser may contact Google Fonts for typography, cdnjs and jsDelivr for pinned libraries, Element 84 Earth Search for public scene metadata, public Sentinel-2 COG storage to read raster windows, and OpenStreetMap tile servers to render the map. Those providers may receive standard request metadata such as your IP address, browser headers, requested URLs, and timestamps according to their own practices.

Area and date information

The selected rectangle and date range are sent to Earth Search to locate imagery. The browser reads the relevant cloud-optimized raster windows from the selected scenes. Your study area name is used locally in the interface and exported files; it is not included in the STAC query. Do not use personal, confidential, or sensitive labels.

Local processing and exports

Results are computed in the browser. HTML and CSV exports are created locally when you choose to download them. Your browser and operating system may keep downloaded files according to your own settings. This site does not upload those exports.

Cookies, tracking, and changes

The site does not set its own cookies or use analytics at the time of writing. External providers may use their own technical mechanisms when you request their resources. If analytics, account features, or other integrations are added later, this notice must be updated before deployment.
