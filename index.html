SATELLITE CHANGE ANALYSIS

Compare satellite observations. Measure what changed.

Choose an area and two dates. TerraTrace searches Sentinel-2 Level-2A scenes, compares vegetation, surface-water and built-surface indices, maps the difference, and prepares a report. Sample data is available separately for demonstrations.

03 / METHOD

What this comparison can and cannot tell you

TerraTrace compares spectral indices from the least-cloudy Sentinel-2 Level-2A scene found for each selected date. A date may have no suitable scene.

Data source

Element 84 Earth Search STAC API. Sentinel-2 surface-reflectance COG assets are read in the browser for the selected area. The least-cloudy scene is selected independently for each date within a configurable search window.

Indices

NDVI uses B08 and B04. NDWI uses B03 and B08. MNDWI uses B03 and B11. NDBI uses B11 and B08. NDWI and MNDWI are spectral indicators, not direct measurements of water quantity or quality.

Area estimates

Class areas are calculated from the projected pixel size and valid pixel counts. Output is sampled to a maximum of 256 by 256 pixels. Mixed 10 m and 20 m bands are resampled to a common grid for comparison.

Limitations

Cloud masking is approximate without a full scene-classification mask. Differences can reflect seasonality, atmosphere, viewing geometry, mixed pixels or processing artifacts. Results require expert review before operational use.
