THE ANALYSIS PIPELINE

From source data to a reviewable signal

CONCEPTUAL WORKFLOW
  1. Data inputsSatellite imagery, rainfall and terrain (planned)
  2. Quality checksCoverage, consistency and missing data
  3. IndicatorsNDVI, NDWI, NDBI and land-surface temperature
  4. Change reviewSeparate simulated signals from hypotheses
  5. Human reviewValidate independently before decisions

PLANNED DATA SOURCES

What a real version could use

  • Sentinel-2Optical imagery for vegetation, land cover and surface-water context.
  • LandsatLonger-term land-surface observation and thermal products, subject to product availability.
  • CHIRPS rainfallGridded precipitation estimates to add rainfall context.
  • SRTM terrainElevation and derived terrain context for screening.

These are proposed integrations, not active connections in this demo.

LIMITATIONS

What this prototype cannot claim

  • All current data is synthetic, not measured at the named study area.
  • Surface-water indicators do not measure groundwater availability or water quality.
  • Land-surface temperature (LST) is not air temperature.
  • Indicator changes alone do not prove global warming or causation.
  • SolarScope is early screening, not engineering design or energy-yield prediction.
  • Hypotheses and priority scores need independent validation and local context.
